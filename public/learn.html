About STTYTT

STTYTT was founded with the objective of creating innovative products and redefining everyday technology into world-class solutions.

Our mission is to build sustainable electric mobility solutions that provide better performance, reliability, affordability and luxury without compromising the environment.

From restoring vehicles, modifying race cars, developing biodegradable products and running successful businesses, STTYTT has continuously evolved into a technology company focused on the future of electric transportation.
